An anchored overlay (a select or picker panel, a menu, a popover, a tooltip) whose anchor has scrolled out of view, past the top or bottom of the viewport or out of a scrolling container, now scrolls away with it on the side it was on, instead of being pushed into the viewport and pinned to its edge over unrelated content. While the anchor is at least partly visible it still flips, shrinks to the room and shifts into view as before, and near the left or right edge it keeps its horizontal place as it leaves. It does not close: when the anchor scrolls back the overlay is next to it again, with focus and ARIA state untouched.

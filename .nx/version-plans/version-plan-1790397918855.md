AndesDatePicker's default size is now 32px high with 14px text (sm 24px, lg 40px).

In the dark theme a selected range, day hover and preset/time hover are visible again (they used the popover color itself), and the today outline meets 3:1 contrast.

AndesDatePicker gains `picker="time"` for a time of day alone (time columns without a calendar, in single or range mode), `[(value)]` binding without Angular forms, public `focus()`/`blur()` methods, `timeDisabled` (with `hideDisabledOptions`), 12-hour time columns (`use12Hours`), `showTime.defaultTime`, `order` for time ranges that cross midnight, `preserveInvalidOnBlur`, a hover preview of days and presets in the input (`previewValue`), `panelTemplate` to wrap the panel, `clearIcon`, calendar icon templates and `panelView`. AndesCalendar gains `panelView`, `fullCellTemplate`, `cellSelect` (each pick with the grid it came from), `cellHover`, previous/next icon templates, and `dateDisabled` now receives the range start as `info.from` while the end is picked.

AndesDatePicker's field now follows the shared field look: a tinted border on hover and, on focus or while open, a primary border with a soft 2px ring in place of the outline around the border (red or amber with a status); disabled fields are muted instead of faded, and a range's active input is marked by a 2px bar on the field's bottom edge. The panel always opens below or above the field, never beside it, and fades in from 97% scale over 150ms. AndesCalendar's weekday headers are now 14px, like its day numbers.

AndesDatePicker gains range options: `disabled` takes a `[start, end]` pair that locks one side (read-only, out of the Tab order, `aria-disabled`, never moved by the calendar), with `disabledSides` for the same next to a forms directive; `allowEmpty` commits open-ended ranges (`[start, null]`, `[null, end]`) through `valueChange`, forms, formats and `needConfirm`; and `[(activePickerIndex)]` reports and sets the active input. `mode="multiple"` selects any number of dates as a `Date[]`, shown as removable tags (Backspace removes the last, Enter adds a typed date), with `maxTagCount` (a count or `responsive`) collapsing the rest into a `+ N ...` tag whose hover, focus or click popover lists them, `maxCount`, `needConfirm` and every calendar picker. New `defaultOpen`, `autoFocus`, `panelClass` and `changeOnBlur` (commit typed text and pending picks on blur, even with `needConfirm`) inputs; a hovered day is previewed only after the pointer moves over the panel, and an RTL field mirrors its panel.

AndesCalendar gains `mode="multiple"` with `maxCount`, a per-side `disabled` pair and `editingSide` for ranges (open-ended `{ start: null, end }` values included), and `captionLayout` (`dropdown`, `dropdown-months`, `dropdown-years`): labelled month and year `AndesSelect`s (small, borderless, their lists stacked above the date picker's panel) bounded by `min`/`max` or `startYear`/`endYear`, one set per visible month, which `AndesDatePicker` passes through.

AndesDatePicker's panel has the same transparent edge and soft elevation as Select and Combobox, and grows from the edge facing the field: `data-side` and `data-align` report the side and the edge it really took (also after the overlay flips it), and `dir="rtl"` mirrors them. All its motion reads the shared motion tokens. `fixedWeeks` is now on by default, so the panel stays six rows tall from month to month (`[fixedWeeks]="false"` shrinks it again), the clear button cross-fades with the calendar icon, and a range marks its active input with one bar that slides to the other input in 300ms, with an arrow on the panel that follows it.

AndesDatePicker's time columns: the field and the columns share one clock, 24 hours unless `showTime.use12Hours` asks for AM/PM (the field used to print 12-hour text beside 00-23 columns). A picked time scrolls to the top of its column, at once when the panel opens and eased afterwards, every option can reach the top, hovering fades, Left and Right move between columns and PageUp and PageDown jump five options.

AndesCalendar's range preview runs as one dashed band from the picked start to the hovered day, with no gap beside the start (cells gain `previewStart` and `previewEnd`). The calendar and the date picker gain forced-colors styles for the field, days, selection, range, today, focus and time columns; in a right-to-left grid the Left and Right keys and the default chevrons mirror; and one polite live region announces the months on show instead of one per caption.

AndesDatePicker gains the rest of its date and time picker features. `format` takes `{ format, type: 'mask' }` to type through a mask (digits fill the number under the caret, Left/Right switch numbers, Up/Down step one, a half-typed mask goes back to the value on blur); a token `format` with time tokens decides the time columns (`HH:mm`, `HH`, `h:mm a`), and `showTime` gains `showHour`, `showMinute` and `changeOnScroll`. New `pickerValue` (with `pickerValueChange`, and `defaultMonth` also taking a range's pair), `timeCellTemplate`, `tagTemplate`, `headerTemplate` and `fullCellTemplate` (passed to the calendar), a template `rangeSeparator`, `id` as `{ start, end }`, an `extraFooter` context holding the grid on show, `valueTextChange` (the committed value in the display format) and `focused`/`blurred` with the side. Hovering a time option previews it in the input. `calendarChange` now emits `{ value, text, range }` instead of a bare `[start, end]` tuple, and also fires for times picked in a range.

AndesCalendar gains a controlled `month` (`[(month)]`), reports a click on a leading or trailing day through `monthChange` and `panelChange`, and a year picked on the way to a day now opens that year's days instead of stopping at the month grid. The date formats gain the Buddhist-era tokens `BBBB` and `BB`, and a locale on the Buddhist calendar (`th-TH`, `-u-ca-buddhist`) now reads its typed year back.

Add AndesTreeSelect, a select whose options form a tree: single, multiple (removable tags with `maxTagCount`) and checkable selection with `showCheckedStrategy`, search in the field or the panel, lazy loading, field renaming, loading and empty states, and a panel that always opens above or below the field. It works with forms and `andes-form-field`. It also offers `[(open)]` and `defaultOpen`, `panelClass`, `expandAction`, a numeric `matchTriggerWidth`, `labelInValue`, the `selectionChange`, `optionSelect`, `optionDeselect` and `panelScroll` outputs, `panelHeader` and `panelFooter`, and the `tagTemplate`, `prefix`, `suffixIcon`, `clearIcon`, `removeIcon` and `loadingIcon` templates, and the field names the tree itself in `aria-controls`. `maxTagCount="responsive"` keeps the tags on one row, the "+ N" tag opens a popover that lists and removes the hidden ones (`maxTagPopover`), and `maxTagTextLength` cuts long tags short. `maxCount` caps a multiple or checkable selection, disabling the other nodes once it is reached. `autoClearSearchValue` (on by default, as in the select) empties the search after a pick in a multiple select. ArrowUp opens the panel too, and a live region announces the tags the user adds or removes. The clear button cross-fades with the chevron or spinner instead of replacing it. A tag the user adds grows in briefly.

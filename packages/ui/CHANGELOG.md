## 0.3.0 (2026-11-15)

### 🚀 Features

- Upgrade `andes-button` and `andes-button-group`. ([1044bf94f](https://github.com/andes-ng/andes/commit/1044bf94f))

  Press feedback: while pressed a button scales a little (`--andes-button-press-scale`, 0.98 by default; above 1 it grows) with a short ease (`--andes-button-press-duration`, `--andes-button-press-ease`). Disabled and loading buttons, link-style buttons and users who prefer reduced motion get none, and the new `pressScale` input turns it off. The new `elevation` input (`low`, `medium`, `high`) raises a floating button on the shadow tokens.

  Async and focus: `clickAction` runs on the click and keeps the button loading until the promise it returns settles (rejections go to `ErrorHandler`), `interruptible` keeps it clickable meanwhile so only the newest action ends the loading state, and `focusableWhenDisabled` keeps a disabled or loading button in the tab order with `aria-disabled` while clicks stay blocked. A loading button announces `loadingLabel` through a polite status region, an icon-only button without an accessible name warns in development, and `focus()` moves focus to the real button. Form and link attributes: `form`, `name`, `value` and `rel`.

  `andes-button-group` gains `disabled` (every button at once), `elevation` (the joined buttons share one shadow) and `roving` (one tab stop, arrow keys along the orientation, Home and End, right-to-left aware). The button stories are regrouped by topic, and the button primitive gains `focusable` and `tabStop` inputs.

- Upgrade `andes-steps` and `andes-step` with a new `segments` type, a `density` input, indicator modes, warning and loading statuses, end content and a collapsed summary. Every addition is optional and the existing inputs keep their behaviour. ([a723799db](https://github.com/andes-ng/andes/commit/a723799db))

  The `segments` type (`type="segments"`) draws a thin rounded progress bar above every step, filled for finished and current steps and dim for upcoming ones, then the step's indicator and title: a filled disc with a check when done, a ring while current, the small muted number ahead, a cross for an error. The current step rests in a soft rounded pill. It works horizontally and stacked (the bar runs down the start edge), in the default and small sizes, in the light and the dark theme and right to left, cuts a long title with an ellipsis, fills the current bar by `percent`, keeps the clickable roving tab stop, and reads `--andes-steps-segment-accent`, `--andes-steps-segment-accent-foreground`, `--andes-steps-segment-track`, `--andes-steps-segment-height`, `--andes-steps-segment-gap` and `--andes-steps-pill-background`. The bar is the step's `tail` and its fill the `rail`, so `classNames` and `styles` reach it. It honours reduced motion and forced colours.

  When a `segments` trail gets less than `minimumStepWidth` (112px) per step it collapses, according to `collapsedVariant`: `summary-controls` (the default for this type; bars, the current step and its place, and previous and next buttons that skip disabled steps when the trail is `clickable`), `summary`, `track` (bars only) or `stack` (turn vertical like the other types). Every step keeps its name in the accessible list. The new `summary` part of `classNames` and `styles` styles the row, and the labels gain `previous` and `next`.

  `indicator` (`auto`, `number` or `none`, on the trail and overridable per step) picks what every marker shows. `density` (`compact`, `default` or `comfortable`) sets the gap between stacked steps and the padding of navigation cells and segment pills. The status gains `warning` (an amber triangle, amber title and bar, announced as Warning) and `loading` (a turning ring, announced as busy with `aria-busy`), in every type, variant and orientation; `labels` gains `warning` and `loading`. A step takes `endContent` (text or a template) at the end of its title row, with a new `end` part for `classNames` and `styles`.

- Skeleton: the shimmer gets a `direction` (`horizontal`, the default, sweeps in reading direction and flips under right-to-left; `vertical` travels from top to bottom in every document direction) and a `reverse` switch that runs either sweep the other way, on `andes-skeleton` and on every standalone placeholder. The new `stagger` input of `andes-skeleton` and `andes-skeleton-text` (milliseconds between two bars, a bare attribute is 100) starts each bar a step after the one above it so a card or a list ripples from the top down, and the `index` input of the standalone placeholders and of the single bar does the same for hand-built groups; `--andes-skeleton-stagger` and `--andes-skeleton-delay` tune the step and the wait for a whole subtree. The new `radius` input (`none`, `sm`, `md`, `lg`, `xl`, `full`) sets the corners of the bars. All of it is dropped under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the `classNames` and `styles` functions now also receive `direction`, `reverse`, `stagger`, `index` and `radius`. ([c25e52c77](https://github.com/andes-ng/andes/commit/c25e52c77))
- Add `andes-carousel` and `andes-carousel-slide`: a slide show with scroll, fade and slide-fade transitions (`effect`, or the `fade` shorthand, with `speed`, `easing` and `waitForAnimate`), seamless looping, several slides per view (breakpoint `slidesPerView`, `slidesToScroll`, `align`, `peek`, `containScroll`), vertical mode, drag (`draggable`, `dragFree`, `dragThreshold`, `skipSnaps`), wheel scrolling, keyboard navigation, `adaptiveHeight`, `aspectRatio`, `startIndex` and lazily rendered far slides, following the accessible carousel pattern. ([0f2ca7288](https://github.com/andes-ng/andes/commit/0f2ca7288))

  Autoplay is an explicit state machine (running, or paused by hover, keyboard focus, a drag, the user, a hidden tab or scrolling out of view, each resuming on its own with a full delay; reduced motion turns it off) with a play and pause button, an options object (`dotProgress` fills the active dot), `autoplayState` and `isPlaying`. Dots come in `dot`, `line` and `pill` styles and several sizes, can sit on all four edges inside or outside the slides, and take colour custom properties. Arrows can be inside, outside or custom, the `andes-carousel-thumbs` strip follows the slide, and the `selectedIndex`, `slideCount`, `canScrollPrev` and `canScrollNext` signals and the `slideChange`, `slideChanged`, `dragStarted`, `dragEnded`, `settled` and `ready` outputs report the state.

  Add `andes-carousel-scroller` and `andes-carousel-scroller-item`, a native-scrolling strip for cards and tiles: optional scroll snapping (`snap`), fading edges (`fadeEdges`, width from `--andes-carousel-fade-size`), previous and next buttons that appear only while the row can move that way (`arrows`, `arrowSize`), `loop` from the last item back to the first, `paging` (`item` or `view`) so a touch or pen flick, a horizontal trackpad swipe and Shift plus the wheel move one item or one view while the mouse wheel keeps the native snapping, `gap` and `itemWidth` (also `--andes-carousel-gap` and `--andes-carousel-item-width`), `reducedMotion`, `labels`, RTL, a labelled carousel region (a development warning when the name is missing), items announced as "slide, n of m", a scroll container that is a tab stop only while the items overflow (arrow keys, Home and End move it while snapping) and the `overflowing`, `canScrollPrev`, `canScrollNext`, `next()`, `prev()` and `scrollToItem()` members.

- AndesPagination: sizes small, default (also `medium`) and large (40px), a plain-text function form of `showTotal`, a `sizeChangerRender` template to replace the built-in page-size select, numeric-string `pageSizeOptions`, a localizable `jumpToSuffixLabel` after the quick jumper input, and a go button that confirms the page input in editable simple mode. The page-size select is as wide as its longest option, so it neither jumps as the size changes nor stays wider than its text. ([959804722](https://github.com/andes-ng/andes/commit/959804722))

  AndesPagination keeps keyboard focus inside the control by moving it to the current page when the activated Previous / Next button becomes disabled or a jump-ellipsis button is removed.

  AndesPagination: new `layout` input (`default`, `compact` for previous / "Page 2 of 10" / next, and `table` for the data-table footer with "Rows per page", the item range and first / previous / next / last buttons), `variant` (`default`, `outline`, `ghost`, `filled`) and `shape` (`default`, `pill`), `showFirstLast`, `prevText` / `nextText` with `showNavIcons`, `hasNextPage` for a cursor-style control with no known total, `loading`, `linkFor` to draw the items as links, `classNames` and `styles` for each part (objects or functions of the control's state), `jumperIcon`, `defaultCurrent` / `defaultPageSize`, and localisable `firstLabel`, `lastLabel`, `rowsPerPageLabel`, `statusLabel` and `rangeLabel`.

  AndesPagination now draws every item (pages, previous, next, first, last and the jump links) with the library button, which is a link when `linkFor` gives the page an address, and its quick jumper and simple-mode page field with the library input; a new `color` input sets the colour that marks the current page.

  AndesButton: new `aria-current` input, written on the real button or link. AndesInput: new `inputMode` input, written on the native field.

  AndesPagination now announces the page and the item range after each change, moves focus along its items with the arrow keys, Home and End, marks a quick jumper that holds text that is not a page number as invalid, and uses the library select for the page-size changer.

  AndesPagination items are 4px, 6px and 8px apart (small, default, large) with an 8px radius, and the page-size and jump-to fields sit 24px after the pages.

  The Pagination stories are now grouped by topic (overview, sizes and variants, navigation, page size, total and ranges, simple and compact, state and control, customisation, accessibility, compositions), one story per example.

  AndesPagination items no longer animate their selected look: the current page changes at once, with no fade that left the old page half highlighted, and a hovered plain item fades its tint in over 80ms. Jump items cross-fade their ellipsis and double chevrons in 150ms, every item shares one centre line, loading disables every control but the current page and keeps the last total, and the total text is muted.

- Added the tabs: `AndesTabs` with `andes-tabs-list`, `andes-tabs-trigger` and `andes-tabs-content`, or a data-driven `items` list. Five types: `line` (an ink bar), `card`, `editable-card` (add and close tabs, per-tab `closable`, `hideAdd`, custom icons), `pill` (a tinted thumb behind the active tab) and `segmented` (a muted track under a raised thumb).   Tabs can sit at the top, bottom, start or end (a start or end bar moves to the top below 480px through `collapseBelow`), come in small, medium and large sizes, be centred, `stretch` over the bar, carry a lucide icon, a template label or an `ariaLabel` for an icon-only tab, and be disabled (a disabled tab lets the pointer through to its wrapper so a tooltip can say why). Panels are lazy or kept alive, and `animated.tabPane` can cross-fade or slide (mirrored in right-to-left). `classNames` and `styles` reach the root, bar, list, trigger, indicator, panels, content, extra, more, moreMenu and add parts, as objects or functions of the tabs' props.   The stories are grouped by topic (overview, types, placement and size, indicator, overflow, extra content and custom bar, customisation, accessibility, compositions and real world) with one story per example.

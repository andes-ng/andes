Add AndesSlider: a single-value and two-handle range slider with min/max/step (and marks-only stepping), labelled marks and tick dots, vertical orientation, reversible and RTL-aware axes, pointer drag, full ARIA APG keyboard support, a value tooltip, and ControlValueAccessor for both forms APIs.

AndesSlider joins the reworked form-control family. `name` renders one hidden input per handle (a range repeats the name once per handle, in handle order) so native forms and `FormData` carry the value. `minDistance` keeps neighbouring handles apart for dragging, the keyboard, rail and mark clicks and added handles, aligned to the `step` grid. `status` (`error` | `warning`) and `aria-invalid` paint the slider and mark every handle invalid, `readOnly` keeps the handles focusable and announced (`aria-readonly`) without hover or press feedback, and `size` (`sm` | `md` | `lg`) scales the rail and thumb. `thumbTemplate` replaces the thumb circle with a template (`$implicit` value, `index`, `dragging`, `focused`), `focus(index)` / `blur()` gain `focused` and `blurred` outputs, and `dragStart` / `dragEnd` outputs report pointer drags (index -1 for a `draggableTrack` range). `trackClick` turns off rail presses, and `snapToMarks` with `snapThreshold` makes a pointer drag stick to nearby marks. It also gains forced-colors support.

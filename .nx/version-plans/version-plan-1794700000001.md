Skeleton: the shimmer gets a `direction` (`horizontal`, the default, sweeps in reading direction and flips under right-to-left; `vertical` travels from top to bottom in every document direction) and a `reverse` switch that runs either sweep the other way, on `andes-skeleton` and on every standalone placeholder. The new `stagger` input of `andes-skeleton` and `andes-skeleton-text` (milliseconds between two bars, a bare attribute is 100) starts each bar a step after the one above it so a card or a list ripples from the top down, and the `index` input of the standalone placeholders and of the single bar does the same for hand-built groups; `--andes-skeleton-stagger` and `--andes-skeleton-delay` tune the step and the wait for a whole subtree. The new `radius` input (`none`, `sm`, `md`, `lg`, `xl`, `full`) sets the corners of the bars. All of it is dropped under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the `classNames` and `styles` functions now also receive `direction`, `reverse`, `stagger`, `index` and `radius`.

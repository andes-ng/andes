The library's controls read the enclosing `andes-form-field` and `andesForm` through `injectAndesFieldBinding()`, with or without `andesFormControl`: placed in a field, a control takes the field's id (so the label points at it), is named by the label (`aria-labelledby`) unless it has its own name, is described by the field's hint and messages (merged with its own `aria-describedby`), shows the field's error or warning (`aria-invalid` where its role has it, its status otherwise), announces a required field, and registers with the field so the label's mark and the messages follow its validators. Each control also takes the form's `size` (and `variant`, where it has the same vocabulary) when its own is unset, and is disabled with the form. Their `size` (and `variant`) inputs now default to `undefined`, read the value in effect from the new `resolvedSize()` (and `resolvedVariant()`, `resolvedStatus()`).

Text inputs: `AndesInput`, `AndesInputPassword`, `AndesInputSearch`, `AndesInputOtp` (which gains `required`, announced with `aria-required` on its cells), `AndesTextarea` and `AndesInputNumber`.

Choice controls: `AndesCheckbox`, `AndesCheckboxGroup`, `AndesRadioGroup`, `AndesSwitch`, `AndesSegmented`, `AndesToggle` and `AndesToggleGroup`. A checkbox, switch or toggle with no text of its own is named by the field label. The groups (`checkbox-group`, `radio-group`, `segmented`, `toggle-group`) gain `id` and `aria-describedby` inputs and take the field's id and description on their group element; their items stay out of the field and show the group's error status. `AndesSegmented` takes the form's `outlined` / `filled` / `borderless` variant; `AndesToggle` gains `id` and `required` (`data-required`); `AndesRadioGroup` reflects its size as `data-size`. `AndesToggleGroupApi` gains `resolvedSize` and `resolvedStatus`.

Popup controls: `AndesSelect`, `AndesCombobox` and `AndesColorPicker`. Clicking the field label focuses their trigger without opening it. `AndesCombobox` gains an `aria-describedby` input; `AndesColorPicker` gains `required` (`data-required`) and keeps its panel's own fields out of the enclosing field (`provideAndesFieldBoundary()`). Range controls: `AndesSlider` (which gains `id` and `required`, as `data-required`) and `AndesRate` name their `role="slider"` element after the field label. `AndesButton` takes the form's size when its own and its group's are unset, and is disabled with the form, so a submit button follows the form it submits; it never takes a field's id.

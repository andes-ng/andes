AndesTag now draws the three variants the way Ant Design does: `outlined` is a soft wash of the color with a border, `filled` is the same wash with no border, `solid` is the saturated fill. A raw CSS color keeps a full-strength outline; named hues keep a soft one. An unchecked checkable tag is bare text until hovered or checked, and a disabled tag turns grey in every color instead of fading. Icons sit 7px from the label (the close button 3px, or 7px after an icon), the close glyph is 10px and half strength until hovered, and a projected `<a>` label fills the whole tag.

AndesTag gains `classNames` and `styles` (objects, or functions of the tag's props) for its `root`, `icon` and `content` parts, `closeIcon` accepts `true` for the default glyph as well as a template, and `avatar` shows a small round picture before the label. A bare `<svg>` icon with its own `width`/`height` keeps that size and stays centred. The palette gains the `volcano`, `gold` and `geekblue` hues.

AndesCheckableTagGroup gains `classNames` and `styles` for its `root` and each option's `item` tag, and options accept a `style`.

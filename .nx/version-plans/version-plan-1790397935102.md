AndesCheckbox: the box is now 16px with 14px label text.

The unchecked box border now meets 3:1 contrast in both themes, the box lines up with the first line of a wrapping label, and an invalid box keeps its danger border on hover.

AndesCheckbox gains `focused`/`blurred` outputs and a `nativeElement` getter for the underlying input, accepts a number `value`, and exposes `--andes-checkbox-border-width` for the box outline. AndesCheckboxGroup accepts number values and numeric `options` (the selection stays numeric), and option objects can carry a `className` and `style` for the rendered checkbox.

AndesCheckbox fades its check mark in just behind the box fill (no transition under reduced motion).

AndesCheckbox gains `size` (`sm` / `md` / `lg`, the Input's control scale), `status` (`error`, which also sets `aria-invalid`, or `warning`), `variant` (`card` for a bordered, fully clickable card; `button` for a toggle button), `indicator` to show or hide the box, and `checkedIcon` / `indeterminateIcon` templates. Text in `[slot=description]` renders muted under the label and is linked with `aria-describedby`. The pointer target is at least 24px (44px on touch screens). Checking now draws the tick and pops the box, the indeterminate dash scales in and unchecking fades out; nothing animates on first render, under reduced motion or in forced-colors mode.

AndesCheckboxGroup gains `readOnly`, `required` (with `aria-required` and form validation), `minCount` / `maxCount` with a `limitReached` output (select-all respects both), `compareWith` for object values, `columns` for a grid layout, and `size`, `status`, `variant`, `indicator` and icon inputs its items inherit. `variant="button"` joins the items into one segmented control. Options accept a `description` and their own `variant`. `AndesCheckboxValue` now includes objects, and `orientation` defaults to horizontal for a button group.

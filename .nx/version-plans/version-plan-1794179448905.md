AndesTabs gets two more types, `pill` (a muted rounded track with the active tab raised out of it) and `segmented` (joined, outlined buttons with the active one filled), plus `stretch` for tabs that share the whole bar, `collapseBelow` (a start or end bar moves to the top below 480px, or `null` to keep it), a `slide` option for `animated.tabPane`, `indicator.thickness`, `ariaLabel` on a tab for an icon-only tab, and `classNames` and `styles` for the root, bar, list, trigger, indicator, panels, content, extra, more, moreMenu and add parts, as objects or functions of the tabs' props. The `--andes-tabs-indicator-color`, `--andes-tabs-indicator-size`, `--andes-tabs-pill-track-background` and `--andes-tabs-pill-selected-background` custom properties retune a tab set without a class.

The tabs are slimmer: every type is 28px, 36px or 44px tall in the small, medium and large sizes, with a 14px/20px label (16px/24px on large), a 24px gap between line tabs, a 2px gap between card tabs and 12px before the panel. A disabled tab now lets the pointer through to its wrapper so a tooltip can say why it is off.

The tabs stories are grouped by topic (overview, types, placement and size, indicator, overflow, extra content and custom bar, customisation, accessibility, compositions and real world) with one story per example.

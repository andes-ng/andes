AndesCombobox's default size is now 32px high with 14px text (sm 24px, lg 40px). It also gains `freeText` (the typed text becomes the value), a `<textarea>` as the field, `[groupLabelTemplate]` for custom group headings, `[popupTemplate]` for extra content around the suggestion list, and the `(inputFocus)`, `(inputBlur)` and `(inputKeydown)` outputs; pressing anywhere in the popup now keeps the input focused.

The field now shows a single focus indicator - its border in the focus colour plus a soft 2px ring, on any focus - instead of an outline drawn outside the border, and tints its border on hover; options are 14px/22px in 32px rows, and the popup fades, scales and slides 4px toward the field over 150ms as it opens.

The clear button now has one design shared with the other fields - a small muted disc with the "x" knocked out, 1em across with a 24px hit area at the field's own inline padding, shown while the field is hovered or focused (always on touch screens) - and is now a Tab stop of its own with a focus ring, still named by `clearLabel`; AndesCombobox also emits `(focused)` / `(blurred)` alongside `(inputFocus)` / `(inputBlur)`, and the popup animates out (120ms fade to 97% scale and a 4px slide back, mirroring its entrance) when the overlay marks it closing, instantly with reduced motion.

The popup now opens with no option highlighted - by focus, click or code, even with `autoHighlight` - so nothing looks hovered under a pointer that has not moved and Enter cannot pick an unseen option. An option highlights when the pointer actually moves over it (a resting pointer under a list that appears or re-filters, and touch, no longer count; leaving the list drops it), when a navigation key moves the cursor (the first ArrowDown/ArrowUp lands on the selected option, and opens a closed popup with the cursor placed), or - with `autoHighlight` - on the first match once the user types. Reopening a filled field lists every option with the selected one scrolled into view instead of only that one. Options get one look per state, shared with AndesSelect and visible in both themes: highlighted, selected (primary tint, semibold, check), selected and highlighted (a stronger tint), disabled, with outlines in forced-colors mode; consecutive selected options join into one block. The popup closes once its field scrolls out of view, its `start`/`end` alignment and text direction follow a right-to-left field, and it enters over 260ms with a 3px drift from 98% scale and exits over 200ms, easing both ends.

AndesCombobox also gains: `multiple` with `[(values)]` (chips with remove buttons, Backspace removes the last one, the list stays open), `maxCount`, `maxTagCount` with a "+ N" chip and `removeTagLabel`; `allowCreate` with `createLabel` and `(optionCreate)`; `(optionDeselect)`; `defaultOpen`; `panelClass` (its base look now has zero specificity); `id` (on the input, not the host), `name` (hidden inputs for native forms) and `required`; `prefix` and `suffixIcon`; `searchDebounce`; `selectOnBlur`; PageUp/PageDown, and Home/End while an option is highlighted; a second Escape that clears the field with `allowClear`; and an `andesComboboxField` wrapper projected as the field. A projected input now keeps its own `aria-label`/`aria-labelledby` unless the root sets one.

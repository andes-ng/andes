The dialog follows Ant Design's Modal: a 520px surface padded 20px by 24px, an 8px gap under the header and 12px above the footer, a 32px close control, a 45% black mask, zoom-and-fade motion, and a four-bar loading skeleton. New inputs: `fullscreen` (two-way), `maximizable` (a maximize/restore control beside the "x") and `maskClass`, plus the `--andes-dialog-mask` hook for a custom mask fill. Fixes the focus halo of a control next to the pinned header or footer being painted over.

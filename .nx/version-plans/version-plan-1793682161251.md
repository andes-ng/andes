Every icon the components draw is now a lucide icon rendered through `ng-icon`, instead of a hand-drawn inline SVG: chevrons, checks and dashes, the clear and close "x", the status marks, the calendar and clock, the eye of the password toggle, the stepper arrows, stars, the tree's folders, files, grip and line-mode boxes, and the upload and image preview actions. Each component registers the icons it uses, so applications need no `provideIcons` setup; `@ng-icons/core` and `@ng-icons/lucide` (36) are new peer dependencies, never bundled. Icon boxes keep their sizes, colours, RTL mirroring and `aria-hidden`.

The internal icon helpers (`andes-form-icon`, `andes-image-icon`, `andes-upload-icon` and the combobox icon builder) are removed; none was exported from the package. The Empty illustrations stay drawn as they are.

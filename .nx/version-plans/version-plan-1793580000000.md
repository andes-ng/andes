Add `andes-gallery`, an image viewer for a set of pictures with the reference's look: one picture on a dark stage with round previous / next buttons at its sides, a toolbar at the top (zoom with the ratio shown, rotate, flip, reset, download, full screen, and play / pause for a slideshow), a counter, a caption and a thumbnail strip at the bottom, top or in a column at either side that keeps the active thumbnail in view and is one tab stop walked with the arrow keys. It fills its container inline or opens as a lightbox (`[(fullscreen)]`, a modal dialog that grows out of its opener; `[inline]="false"` for a lightbox-only gallery opened from your own grid). Pictures come from `images` (URLs or `{ src, thumbnail, alt, title }`) or `andes-gallery-item` children; `[(activeIndex)]`, `loop`, `autoplay`, `counter`, image / thumbnail / caption / header / footer templates, localised `labels`, `transformChange` and `closed`. It drives the stage with the image viewer's engine (wheel, button and keyboard zoom, drag to pan, double click, pinch, swipe), cross-fades between pictures, preloads the neighbours and falls back on a failed picture. The image preview gains the two-finger pinch zoom.

Added the alert: an inline status message with `info`, `success`, `warning`, `danger` and `neutral` severities, `tinted`, `filled`, `outlined` and `solid` variants, an `md` and a slim `sm` size, a banner and a marquee mode, a title and a description (as inputs or projected content), a custom icon, `actionPlacement` (`bottom` or `end`), and a close button (`closeText`, `closeDisabled`, `closeIcon`) that moves focus on afterwards. A one-line alert is a 40px strip; one with a title and a description is 66px (12px by 16px padding, a 20px icon, a medium 14px title over a softer 13px description). The severity colours follow the theme's status tokens and every glyph, text and close-button colour clears WCAG AA on the light and dark themes. A two-way `open` model shows or hides the alert with the same collapse as the close button, `duration` closes it by itself and holds while it is hovered or focused, and `closing` reports a `reason` (`button` or `timeout`). Parts take `classNames` and `styles`. Icons are lucide, drawn through `ng-icon`.

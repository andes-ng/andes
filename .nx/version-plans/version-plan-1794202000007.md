Added the table: `AndesTable` styles a native `<table>` built from `thead[andesTableHeader]`, `tbody[andesTableBody]`, `tfoot[andesTableFooter]`, `caption[andesTableCaption]`, `tr[andesTableRow]`, `th[andesTableHead]` and `td[andesTableCell]`, so every table keeps its real semantics. It sits in a rounded, soft-bordered frame (`framed`) with rows of 36, 44 or 52px (`density`: compact, default, comfortable), 14px text, roomy padding and an 8px gap between a header title and its icons; `bordered` adds the cell grid, `striped` tints every other row, `rowHover` and `loading` (an overlay with a spinner) are inputs, and the wrapper retunes the row height, the padding, the line and the tint with `--andes-table-row-height`, `--andes-table-cell-padding-block`, `--andes-table-cell-padding-inline`, `--andes-table-line`, `--andes-table-tint` and `--andes-table-stripe`.

Sorting is a `sortKey` on a header, with `sort` and `sorts` (`multiSort`, with the rank drawn beside the arrow), `sortDirections`, sort tooltips and an announcement of every change; the indicator is a lucide arrow for the direction and the up-down pair while the column is unsorted. Column filters (`andes-table-filter`) come as a checkbox menu, a tree, a searchable list, a custom panel and the ready-made text, number range and date range panels with matching `andesTextFilter`, `andesRangeFilter`, `andesDateRangeFilter` and `andesOptionFilter` helpers, `filteredValue`, `defaultFilteredValue`, `applyOnClose`, a custom icon, `(tableChange)`, `clearFilters()`, `clearSort()` and `resetState()`. `andesSortRows`, `andesFilterRows`, `andesPaginateRows`, `andesOrderColumns`, `andesVisibleColumns`, `andesMoveColumn`, `andesFlattenTableTree` and `andesRowsToCsv` prepare the rows.

Rows can be selected (`andes-table-selection`, `andes-table-select-all` with a menu of built-in and custom entries, checkbox or radio, `selectedKeys`, `selectableKeys`, shift ranges and cascading `selectionTree`), expanded (`andes-table-expand-toggle`, `andes-table-expand-all`, `*andesTableExpandedRow`, `expandRowByClick`, nested tables and tree data with `level` and `indent`), and dragged with the CDK. Columns can be fixed to an edge (`fixed`, stacked offsets, edge shadows, `pinIcon`), pinned from `andes-table-column-settings` (`pinnable`, `[(pinned)]`), resized (`resizable`, `minWidth`, `maxWidth`, double click to fit), reordered, hidden and responsive (`hideBelow`). Scrolling comes as `scrollX`, `scrollY`, a page-sticky header, a fixed summary row, `scrollTo` and `*andesTableVirtualRows` for very long lists.

Every control inside the table is a library component: the row and header selection are `andes-checkbox` and `andes-radio`, the select-all menu is the dropdown menu, the toggles and menu buttons are `andes-button`, the filter and column panels are `andes-popover` (checkboxes, radios, `andes-input`, `andes-select`, `andes-date-picker` inside), and a truncated cell or a sortable header shows its text in `andes-tooltip` instead of the browser title. `fill` makes a table take the height of its container, and a resize handle shows a grip while it is hovered or focused.

Also: `cards` turns the rows into labelled lines below a breakpoint, `gridNavigation` makes the table a grid for the keyboard (a roving cell, arrows, Home, End, Page Up and Down, Enter, Escape and Space) with `aria-rowcount` and `aria-colcount` (`rowCount` for paged data), `classNames` and `styles` reach each part as objects or functions of the table's state, `AndesTableEmpty` draws an `andes-empty`, the toolbar slots `andesTableTop` and `andesTableBottom` hold the title, the actions and the pager (`andes-pagination` in its `table` layout), and the table prints on whole pages without its controls.

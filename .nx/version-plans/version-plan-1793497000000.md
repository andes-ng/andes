Add `AndesOrgChart` (`andes-org-chart`), an organization chart on a pannable, zoomable canvas. People are quiet rounded cards (avatar or initials, name, role) on a canvas one step darker than the cards, joined by thin elbow connectors and laid out as a tidy tree of any card size, vertical or horizontal, reversed and right to left, with compact or hanging teams of leaves, assistants beside the stem and dotted-line links. A small round toggle on the card edge expands and collapses each team with an animation (`collapsible` turns it off and keeps every team open; `toggleIconTemplate` swaps its icon and `toggleCount` adds a small report count); reports can be loaded on demand with `loadChildren`. Selection is `none`, `single`, `multiple` or `checkbox` (a cascading checkbox per card with mixed managers and `partiallySelected`) through `[(selected)]`, plus `[(expanded)]`, search that highlights the path to the root, elbow, straight or curved connectors, templates for the card, toggle, toolbar, empty state and connectors, floating zoom controls, an imperative API (`fitView`, `zoomTo`, `scrollToNode`, `expandPath`, `collapseAll`, `exportSvg`), the WAI-ARIA tree keyboard model and CSS custom properties for every color and size. The layout is also exported on its own as `andesOrgChartLayout`.

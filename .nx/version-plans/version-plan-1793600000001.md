Bring AndesTooltip in line with Ant Design's Tooltip, additively.

The tooltip now looks like Ant's: 14px text on 22px lines in a box padded 6px by 8px with a 6px radius, at least 32px tall and 28px wide and at most 250px wide, on an 85% black surface (`#424242` on a dark page) with white text, three soft drop shadows, a 16 x 8 arrow with a rounded tip, 12px between the box and the trigger, and a 120ms zoom-and-fade out of the arrow's tip. The previous theme-following surface is `variant="light"`; `variant="inverse"` is unchanged. A tooltip that would overflow now flips to the opposite side or moves along the trigger, and no longer jumps to a perpendicular side.

New inputs and outputs: `classNames` and `styles` (objects or functions of the tooltip's props, for its `root`, `container` and `arrow` parts), `defaultOpen`, `openChangeDetail` (the new state and its source: hover, focus, click, contextMenu, touch, press, escape-key, outside-click, disabled), a `content` that may be a function, and the Ant Design hue presets for `color` (`pink`, `red`, `yellow`, `orange`, `cyan`, `green`, `blue`, `purple`, `geekblue`, `magenta`, `volcano`, `gold`, `lime`, listed in `ANDES_TOOLTIP_HUE_COLORS`). The text on a custom color or a hue is black or white by its lightness, as in Ant Design.

New `andesTooltipProvider` directive and `provideAndesTooltipConfig()` share `openDelay`, `closeDelay` and `instantReopenWindow` defaults across tooltips (shadcn's `TooltipProvider`), and with `unique` show one tooltip at a time and glide it from trigger to trigger (Ant Design's Smooth Transition). `openDelay`, `closeDelay` and `instantReopenWindow` now default to `undefined`, falling back to the provider and then to the same values as before.

Added the toast: `AndesToastService` for notifications and `AndesMessageService` for short messages, both drawn by one `andes-toast-viewport` that you mount once. Notifications are neutral 360px cards where the severity shows in a lucide icon (or a tinted card with `richColors`), with a title, a description, compact `action`/`cancel` buttons and a corner close button; messages are single-line 9px/12px cards. Toasts stack into a deck that expands and pauses every countdown on hover or focus, move in 200ms (`--andes-toast-duration`), and can be swiped away (`swipeToDismiss`, `swipeDirections`). Options include `showProgress` (a flat 2px countdown line, `--andes-toast-progress-bg`), `richColors`, `gap`, `offset`, `stack`, `visibleToasts`, `stackPeek`, `classNames` and `styles` per part, `titleTemplate`/`messageTemplate`/`actionsTemplate`/`headless` content, `closeIcon`, a per-flavor `rtl`, and `promise()` helpers. The viewport takes `hotkey` (default `Alt+T`, focuses the newest toast) and `container`, and a region holding toasts is a labelled landmark.

Added the tabs: `AndesTabs` with `andes-tabs-list`, `andes-tabs-trigger` and `andes-tabs-content`, or a data-driven `items` list. Five types: `line` (an ink bar), `card`, `editable-card` (add and close tabs, per-tab `closable`, `hideAdd`, custom icons), `pill` (a tinted thumb behind the active tab) and `segmented` (a muted track under a raised thumb). The thumb glides over 300ms in both directions and in vertical and right-to-left bars, scrolls with the tabs, follows tabs that are added, removed, reordered or resized, is placed without sliding the first time, and does not slide under reduced motion; `motion` (`{ duration, easing }`, or `--andes-tabs-duration` and `--andes-tabs-easing`) times it and the line, and `--andes-tabs-track-bg`, `--andes-tabs-thumb-bg`, `--andes-tabs-thumb-border`, `--andes-tabs-thumb-shadow` and `--andes-tabs-thumb-fg` restyle it.

Tabs can sit at the top, bottom, start or end (a start or end bar moves to the top below 480px through `collapseBelow`), come in small, medium and large sizes, be centred, `stretch` over the bar, carry a lucide icon, a template label or an `ariaLabel` for an icon-only tab, and be disabled (a disabled tab lets the pointer through to its wrapper so a tooltip can say why). The indicator takes a size, `thickness` and an alignment (start, center, end). Extra content goes at the start, the end or both sides of the bar. A bar that overflows scrolls with edge fades and arrows and offers a "more" menu of the hidden tabs (`more`, `moreIcon`, `moreLabel`, `moreTrigger` for click or hover, `moreSearch` for a filter field and `moreTemplate` for custom content). Tabs can be reordered by dragging or with `Alt`+`Shift`+arrow keys, reported through the `reorder` output. The tab bar can be sticky and styled (`tabBarStyle`, `tabBarClass`, `tabBarGutter`).

The value is two-way (`value`), with `valueChange`, `tabClick` and `tabScroll` outputs. Panels are lazy or kept alive, and `animated.tabPane` can cross-fade or slide (mirrored in right-to-left). `classNames` and `styles` reach the root, bar, list, trigger, indicator, panels, content, extra, more, moreMenu and add parts, as objects or functions of the tabs' props. Keyboard navigation follows the tablist pattern (arrow keys, Home and End, automatic or manual activation, a roving tab stop) with `tablist`, `tab` and `tabpanel` roles and `aria-orientation`.

Every type is 28px, 36px or 44px tall in the small, medium and large sizes, with a 14px/20px label (16px/24px on large). The pill and segmented segments are 24px, 32px and 40px inside a 2px inset, with 12px, 14px and 16px between the thumb edge and the label; pill tabs sit 4px apart and segmented tabs share the track. Card tabs pad their labels 12px, 14px and 16px, line tabs keep a 24px gap, and the extra content sits 12px from the tabs. Text, selected and resting labels keep WCAG AA contrast on the light and the dark theme. All icons are lucide, drawn through `ng-icon`.

The stories are grouped by topic (overview, types, placement and size, indicator, overflow, extra content and custom bar, customisation, accessibility, compositions and real world) with one story per example.

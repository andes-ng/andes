Add AndesRate, a star rating with whole or half steps, partial fill for read-only averages, hover preview, per-character tooltips, custom characters, slider keyboard semantics and forms integration.

AndesRate paints any value between 0 and `count` proportionally when it is read-only or disabled (4.3 fills 30% of the fifth character, in the reading direction and for custom characters), while an interactive rate draws the nearest whole or half step. It gains `autoFocus` and public `focus()` / `blur()`; `name` (a hidden input for native form posts) and `required` (a value of 0 is a `{ required: true }` forms error, exposed as `data-required` because the slider role has no `aria-required`); `status` (`error` | `warning`, tinting the empty characters and ringing the row, with `error` also announced through `aria-invalid`); `colors` (one colour per position or a function of the highlighted value, with `color` as the single-colour shortcut); and `emptyColor` for the unfilled characters on dark surfaces.

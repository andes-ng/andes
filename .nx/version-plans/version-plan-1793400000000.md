`AndesAlert` now matches Ant Design's Alert: the same sizes (8px by 12px with a 14px icon, 20px by 24px with a 24px icon and a 16px title when there is a description), solid severity glyphs and Ant's light and dark colors for each severity, overridable with `--andes-alert-<severity>-icon`, `-bg` and `-border`. A title-only alert centres its parts on the row, and the new `classNames` and `styles` inputs (an object or a function of the alert's props) style the root, icon, section, title, description, actions and close parts. Closing now squeezes the alert over 0.3s, the marquee runs the other way in right-to-left layouts, and forced-colors mode keeps the outline and the glyph mark.

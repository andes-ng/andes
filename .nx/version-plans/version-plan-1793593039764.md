Skeleton now matches Ant Design's look and sizes. The title and paragraph bars are 16px high with 16px between rows, 24px between the title and the paragraph (28px beside an avatar), and a 12px title offset that centres it on the avatar; an `avatar` is a 40px circle by default and a paragraph of three rows or more ends on a 61% row. Every placeholder shares one 4px corner (a square avatar has none), and the fill is the foreground colour at 6% (12% on the dark theme) instead of a solid token, so it reads on a card in both themes. The `active` shimmer is now a 1.4s `ease` sweep of the same 25/37/63 gradient, still reversed under RTL and dropped under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

New on `andes-skeleton`: `animation` (`shimmer`, `pulse` or `none`; `active` stays the shorthand for `shimmer`), `classNames` and `styles` to add classes and inline styles to the `root`, `header`, `section`, `avatar`, `title` and `paragraph` parts, as objects or as functions of the current inputs, and `active` inside the `avatar` config. The avatar, button, input, image and node placeholders gain `animation`, and `classNames` / `styles` for their `root` and `content` parts. `--andes-skeleton-color` and `--andes-skeleton-highlight-color` recolour the fill and the shimmer band.

`AndesEmpty` now matches Ant Design's Empty: the illustrations and sizes follow Ant's (a 100px picture, 40px for `simple`, 35px when `small`), `classNames` and `styles` reach each part (`root`, `image`, `heading`, `description`, `footer`) as objects or as functions of the resolved state, and a new `heading` input adds a title line. `provideAndesEmpty()` and the `[andesEmptyProvider]` directive set the image, message, per-preset texts (`ANDES_EMPTY_TEXTS_ES` for Spanish) and part styling of every empty state below them, the counterpart of Ant's `ConfigProvider`. `AndesSelect`, `AndesTreeSelect` and `AndesCascader` now show an `andes-empty` (the `select` preset, which follows that provider) in their not-found area instead of the plain text `No data`; their `notFoundContent` still takes text, a template or `null`, and now defaults to `undefined` for the `andes-empty`. `AndesTreeSelect` gains `notFoundContent` (its `labels.empty` is the message of the default state) and `AndesTree` gains `emptyContent`, a template that replaces the tray and the `empty` label of an empty tree (`null` shows nothing).

AndesRadioGroup: the radio circle is now 16px with 14px label text, and button-style options default to 32px high with 14px text (sm 24px, lg 40px), matching AndesButton.

The unselected circle border now meets 3:1 contrast in both themes, the dot is pixel-centered, and the circle lines up with the first line of a wrapping label.

AndesRadio also works on its own outside a group, with a two-way `[(checked)]` model. Generated options accept per-option `class` and `style`, and AndesRadioGroup gains a `blur()` method alongside `focus()`.

AndesRadio selection is softer: when a radio becomes checked its dot scales in over 150ms with the shared ease-out motion tokens while the ring fills, never on first render, and not at all under reduced motion or forced colors.

AndesRadio and AndesRadioGroup now read like AndesCheckbox. `[slot=description]` (or an option's `description`) adds muted secondary text under the label, linked with `aria-describedby` while the accessible name stays the label. `variant="card"` (group, option or standalone radio) makes the whole item a bordered, clickable card that is tinted when checked; `optionType="button"` keeps its own look and wins over it. `status="error" | "warning"` paints the items, and `error` also sets `aria-invalid` on the radiogroup. `readOnly` freezes the selection while staying focusable and announced through `aria-readonly`. `columns` lays the items out in a grid. `indicator` replaces the dot with a template and `showIndicator` hides the circle. `compareWith` matches object values. `loop` (default true) controls whether the arrow keys wrap, and the group gains `focused` and `blurred` outputs for focus entering and leaving it as a whole. Forced colors and dark mode are covered.

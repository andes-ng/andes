Fix what an overlay leaves behind and loses around its exit animation. The overlay no longer keeps the `data-placed-side` mark on content projected from the consumer's view once the pane detaches, so the next opening does not start with the previous side. A Popover and a Popconfirm now come back when their trigger is pressed (or hovered again) while they are still fading out after Escape or a click outside, instead of swallowing that press and needing a second one, and the Tooltip comes back at once, rather than after its open delay, when the pointer returns during its exit.

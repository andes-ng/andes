AndesSwitch: the thumb stays light (and visible) in the dark theme and sits evenly inside the track, unchecked switches get a hover state, read-only and loading switches no longer react to hover, default-size track text is 12px, and reduced motion stops the thumb slide.

AndesSwitch can now be restyled from the host through CSS custom properties: the existing geometry hooks (`--andes-switch-track-height`, `--andes-switch-track-width`, `--andes-switch-thumb-size`, `--andes-switch-track-padding`, `--andes-switch-inner-margin`) now take effect when set on `<andes-switch>` or an ancestor, and new color hooks cover the track (`--andes-switch-track-color`, `--andes-switch-track-hover-color`, `--andes-switch-track-checked-color`, `--andes-switch-track-checked-hover-color`) and the thumb (`--andes-switch-thumb-color`, `--andes-switch-thumb-shadow`).

AndesSwitch motion is calmer: the thumb glides over 200ms with an ease at both ends, the loading spinner turns once a second, an invalid switch shows only its danger focus outline, and small-track text stays at 12px.

AndesSwitch joins the Checkbox and RadioGroup family. A projected label (the default slot or `[slot=label]`) and a `[slot=description]` sit beside the track and toggle it when clicked, with the description linked through `aria-describedby` and the accessible name kept to the label; `labelPosition` (`end` by default, or `start`) follows the writing direction. `variant="card"` turns the row into a bordered, clickable settings card with hover, a focus ring on the card and a highlighted on state, and `status` (`error` or `warning`) paints the danger or warning color, with `error` also setting `aria-invalid`. A new `lg` size (56 x 28px track, 24px thumb) joins `sm` and `md`, which are unchanged.

AndesSwitch can submit with a native form: `name` and `value` (default `on`) render a hidden input while the switch is on, nothing while it is off, and it is disabled together with the switch.

AndesSwitch track content is richer: `checkedChildren` and `unCheckedChildren` accept a string or a `TemplateRef`, and the new `checkedIcon` and `uncheckedIcon` templates are drawn inside the thumb, sized from it and hidden while loading.

AndesSwitch gains a `beforeChange` guard for user toggles: it receives the next state and may answer with a boolean, a `Promise` or an `Observable`. A refusal, throw, rejection or error leaves the switch unchanged and fires the new `changeBlocked` output; while an answer is pending the switch shows `loading`, sets `aria-busy` and ignores further clicks. Form writes and `[checked]` bindings are not guarded. The new `focused` and `blurred` outputs complement `focus()`, `blur()` and `autoFocus`.

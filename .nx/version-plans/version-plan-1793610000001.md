Upload now previews a picture in the image viewer: the file name (and the thumbnail of a `picture` row) opens it over all the pictures of the list, `imagePreview`, `previewConfig` and `previewLabels` tune it, and a file the browser cannot show opens in a new page. `pasteScope` takes files pasted anywhere on the page (`page`) or while the pointer is over the control (`control`). `accept` also takes `{ format, filter }`, `progress` customises the bar (colour, thickness, text, `showInfo`), `classNames` and `styles` reach the parts, `showUploadList` flags can be functions of the file, and `uploadingText` replaces "Uploading...". The list, tiles and drop area are sized like Ant Design's, a failed card keeps its icon and shows the message as its tooltip, rows remove with the trash icon, and the loading bar and spinner move smoothly. A `loading` and an `inbox` icon join the set. The stories gain one example for each Ant Design Upload example, among them a crop dialog with a frame, handles, zoom and rotation.

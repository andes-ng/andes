Add AndesSteps, AndesStep and AndesStepPanel, a progress trail for wizards, onboarding and order tracking, with five types, four marker variants, step panels and a long list of stories.

The trail is horizontal or vertical (collapsing to vertical in a narrow container, or scrolling when `responsive` is off) with the title beside or below the marker, in a default or small size. Its `type` is `default` (numbered markers), `dot` (with an optional dot template), `panel` (one bar of interlocking chevron segments drawn with CSS only: pointed ends and notches, rounded first and last ends, arrows pointing down when stacked and mirrored in right-to-left; the soft and solid variants fill the segments, outline and plain draw the chevron as a line, and the segments show no number or check, only an optional icon, so the status reads from the colours and the hidden status word; fills are mixed over `--andes-steps-surface`), `navigation` (link-like cells with a chevron) or `inline` (compact segments, laid on a grid so `offset` lines short rows up with long ones), and its `variant` is `soft`, `solid`, `outline` or `plain`. Each step derives its status from `current` unless it sets its own, the whole trail can show `status="error"` on the current step, `initial` sets the first number, and `percent` draws a progress ring on the step in progress. Titles, sub titles and descriptions take text or templates, a step can be `optional`, and its `icon` is a lucide icon name or a template; `statusIcons` replaces the marker of every step in a status. The connector is `solid`, `dashed` or `dotted`, fills in 300ms when a step finishes, and a new check or icon enters with a short pop that reduced motion switches off. `maxCount` folds the middle of a long trail into markers that open a list of the hidden steps. `classNames` and `styles` reach the root, item, icon, content, title, sub title, description, tail and rail as objects or functions of the trail and step state. A clickable trail renders buttons with one roving tab stop, arrow keys, Home and End, a `changed` output and a `beforeChange` guard, and has `next()` and `previous()` methods. `andes-step-panel` shows the content of the step in progress, labelled by its title. The trail is an ordered list with `aria-current="step"`, a hidden status word per step and a polite announcement of the new step, and it supports right-to-left, the dark theme and forced colours.

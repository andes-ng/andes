Breadcrumb `[items]` crumbs gain a leading `icon` (with `ariaLabel` for icon-only crumbs, also used in the collapse dropdown), and crumb menus gain dividers, labelled groups, per-entry `key`/`icon`/`danger`/`target`, plus `dropdownProps` (panel `side`/`align`, `panelClass`, `disabled`, `onOpenChange`) and a `(menuOpenChange)` output.

The breadcrumb trail now sits on the 22px body line height instead of the font default.

Breadcrumb links, crumb menu triggers and the collapse dots now hover as a soft rounded pill (no underline) with a pressed state, a focus ring and system colors when colors are forced. The fade is 200ms and is skipped when reduced motion is requested. The current page has no hover.

Breadcrumb `[items]` crumbs gain `target` and `disabled`. A crumb with both a link and a `menu` keeps its link and gets a separate caret button named by the new `menuLabel` input.

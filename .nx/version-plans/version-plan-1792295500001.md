Add `AndesCascader`: a select-like field that opens a panel with one column of options per level, for picking a path through a tree. It supports multiple selection with tags and checkboxes, `changeOnSelect`, click or hover expansion, options loaded on demand with `loadData`, search across every path with highlighted matches, custom display and field names, and tree keyboard navigation (arrows to move, open and close columns, Enter to pick) with a combobox that keeps focus. The pointer highlights an option only after it moves, so a panel opening under a resting pointer shows no highlight, and the cursor stays visible in the dark theme. It also offers `[(open)]` and `defaultOpen`, `name`, `required`, `autoFocus`, `focused` and `blurred`, a two-way `searchValue` with `autoClearSearchValue`, `optionTemplate` applied to search results, `panelHeader`, `panelFooter` and `panelScroll`, and the `tagTemplate`, `prefix`, `suffixIcon`, `clearIcon`, `removeIcon` and `loadingIcon` templates. With `multiple`, an option's check box pops, draws its tick and rings softly when a click changes it, with the checkbox's own motion.

Add `AndesOrgChart` (`andes-org-chart`), an organization chart on a pannable, zoomable canvas. People are cards (avatar or initials, name, role, an accent color per person or per level) laid out as a tidy tree of any card size, vertical or horizontal, reversed and right to left, with compact or hanging teams of leaves, assistants beside the stem and dotted-line links. A count badge expands and collapses each team with an animation; reports can be loaded on demand with `loadChildren`. Single or multiple selection with `[(selected)]`, `[(expanded)]`, search that highlights the path to the root, elbow, straight or curved connectors, templates for the card, badge, toolbar, empty state and connectors, floating zoom controls, an imperative API (`fitView`, `zoomTo`, `scrollToNode`, `expandPath`, `collapseAll`, `exportSvg`), the WAI-ARIA tree keyboard model and CSS custom properties for every color and size. The layout is also exported on its own as `andesOrgChartLayout`.

Breadcrumb gains semantic styling: `[classNames]` and `[styles]` take the parts `root`, `list`, `item`, `link`, `separator`, `ellipsis` and `menu` (the dropdown panel), and reach a hand-written trail as well as `[items]`. The new `AndesBreadcrumbPart`, `AndesBreadcrumbClassNames` and `AndesBreadcrumbStyles` types are exported.

A crumb's `title` can now also be a template, which receives the same context as `[itemRender]`; give it an `ariaLabel` so the collapse dropdown and the menu button have a text name.

Breadcrumb works in right-to-left pages: the dropdown panel repeats the trail's `dir`, and a trail inside a `dir` that differs from the page opens its menus on the edge the trail starts from.

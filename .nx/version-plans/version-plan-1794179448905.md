AndesTabs gets two more types with a gliding thumb: `pill` (a tinted, rounded thumb behind the active tab, whose label takes the primary colour, on a transparent track) and `segmented` (a muted track under a white, raised thumb, like the segmented control). The thumb is measured from the active tab, glides to the new one over 300ms in both directions and in vertical and right-to-left bars, scrolls with the tabs, follows tabs that are added, removed, reordered or resized, is placed without sliding the first time, and does not slide under reduced motion; forced colours map the thumb to the system highlight. The new `motion` input (`{ duration, easing }`, also the `--andes-tabs-duration` and `--andes-tabs-easing` custom properties) times the thumb and the line, `animated.inkBar` switches the slide off, and `--andes-tabs-track-bg`, `--andes-tabs-thumb-bg`, `--andes-tabs-thumb-border`, `--andes-tabs-thumb-shadow` and `--andes-tabs-thumb-fg` restyle it.

AndesTabs also gains `stretch` for tabs that share the whole bar, `collapseBelow` (a start or end bar moves to the top below 480px, or `null` to keep it), a `slide` option for `animated.tabPane`, `indicator.thickness`, `ariaLabel` on a tab for an icon-only tab, and `classNames` and `styles` for the root, bar, list, trigger, indicator, panels, content, extra, more, moreMenu and add parts, as objects or functions of the tabs' props. The `--andes-tabs-indicator-color` and `--andes-tabs-indicator-size` custom properties retune the line without a class.

The tabs are slimmer and roomier: every type is 28px, 36px or 44px tall in the small, medium and large sizes, with a 14px/20px label (16px/24px on large). The pill and segmented segments are 24px, 32px and 40px inside a 2px inset, with 12px, 14px and 16px between the thumb edge and the label; pill tabs sit 4px apart and segmented tabs share the track. Card tabs pad their labels 12px, 14px and 16px, line tabs keep a 24px gap, and the extra content sits 12px from the tabs. A tab brought into view is kept clear of the edge fade, and a disabled tab now lets the pointer through to its wrapper so a tooltip can say why it is off.

The tabs stories are grouped by topic (overview, types, placement and size, indicator, overflow, extra content and custom bar, customisation, accessibility, compositions and real world) with one story per example.

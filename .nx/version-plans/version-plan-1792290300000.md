Add `andes-image`, a picture with lazy loading, placeholder, fallback and error states and an optional full-viewport preview (zoom, rotate, flip, reset, download, drag-to-pan, keyboard shortcuts), and `andes-image-group`, which lets the preview step through several images with a "2 / 5" counter. The placeholder can be a skeleton, a blurred thumbnail, a template or a progress indicator (`{ progress: true }` or `{ progress: { percent } }`); `fallback` takes a URL or a template; the hover mask comes in `dimmed`, `blur` and `none` variants. The viewer has the dark look of the reference (light icons on dark discs, round previous / next buttons at the sides, the counter above a toolbar pill with the zoom ratio), grows out of the clicked thumbnail and cross-fades between images, takes a custom toolbar (`toolbarTemplate`) and custom content (`imageTemplate`), stacks above a dialog it was opened from, and `classNames` / `styles` reach every part. A group's `items` lets one cover open a whole album or show images that have no thumbnail on the page; `andesDrawTransformedImage` draws the rotated and flipped picture for saving it.

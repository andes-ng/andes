AndesFormField's label tooltip trigger now renders in the component font, and `size` on `andesForm` targets the control scale where the default size is 32px high with 14px text (sm 24px, lg 40px).

Error and warning help text, the required mark and the warning feedback icon now meet WCAG AA contrast in both themes: text uses the status text tokens (with the fill as fallback), and the label tooltip matches AndesTooltip's surface.

`andesForm` gains `disabled` (disables and re-enables the bound Angular form), a `variant` hint with `injectAndesFormVariant()` and `injectAndesFormDisabled()` for controls, `(validSubmit)` / `(invalidSubmit)` outputs that report the submit verdict after async validators settle (with every failing control path, also available as `collectAndesFormErrors()`), `scrollToField(path)`, and scroll/focus options for `scrollToFirstError`. Fields and forms gain `showErrorsOn` to choose when status appears (interacted, touched, dirty, submitted or always) and `feedbackIcons` templates; fields also gain non-blocking `warningValidators`, `hidden`, a `data-path` attribute, template support for `tooltip`, and `requiredMark` accepts a template.

Labels are 14px/22px and description, help, extra and error text 12px/20px; vertical labels sit 8px above their control and fields are 24px apart; in horizontal and inline layouts the label centres on the first control row (so it stays at the top beside a tall control), and a focused native control in an error or warning field shows the status border with a soft 2px ring instead of an outline. The status tint now reaches only native controls placed directly in the field, never the inner input of a wrapped control.

`andesFormControl` works with signal forms' `[formField]` (a schema's `hidden()` hides the field), clears the error a failed submit revealed when the form is reset with `resetForm()`, and follows a `[formControl]` re-bound to another control. Its `describedBy()`, `labelledBy()`, `resolvedId()` and `ariaRequired()` return `undefined` instead of `null`, so they bind into the library controls' inputs under strict templates. A field shows a cross-field validator's error from its parent group with `groupErrors`. `andesForm` exposes `submitted` as a signal.

On a screen 575px wide or less a horizontal form stacks its fields: the label moves above the control, at the start, wraps instead of truncating and drops its colon.

Form messages move: a generated message, a help line and an `<andes-form-error>` slide 4px down and fade in over 200ms as they appear, and fold up and fade out over 150ms as they go (the height animates where the browser interpolates `auto`). Reduced motion turns all of it off. A message text produced by two validators is shown once.

`<andes-form-field id>` no longer leaves the id on its host, which duplicated it. Forms and fields gain `messageMode` (`'first'` shows only the first failing validator's message); fields gain `dependsOn` (re-validate when other controls change) and `htmlFor` (point the label elsewhere, or nowhere with `null`); a form with a `name` generates `<name>_<path>` field ids. Clicking a label focuses a popup control (`aria-haspopup`) instead of opening it. `andesForm` gains `submit()`, `reset(value?)`, `(valuesChange)` and `(formReset)`, and `focusFirstError()` / `scrollToField()` skip hidden fields. `<andes-form-error [control]>` shows the own errors of a group or array, and the new `AndesFormFieldset` (`<andes-form-fieldset legend description [control]>`) groups fields under a native fieldset and legend with the group's errors at the bottom; `injectAndesControlErrors()` gives the same state to custom components.

Signal forms are supported end to end: `andesFormControl` on a `[formField]` reports its state, required mark and hidden rule to the field, `andesForm` on a `<form [formRoot]>` emits `validSubmit` / `invalidSubmit` (with each failing field's path) after async rules settle, `valuesChange`, tracks `submitted` and resets with `reset()`. Error messages cover signal forms' `minLength` / `maxLength` kinds and prefer a rule's own `message` over the built-in default.

`AndesValidators` adds the rules `Validators` lacks: `notBlank` (whitespace only), `exactLength(n)`, `url({ protocols })`, `oneOf(values)` and `matchField(path)` (a password confirmation, with `dependsOn`), each with a built-in message.

`injectAndesFieldBinding()` is the control-field contract: a control calls it in its injection context and gets the field's id, `aria-describedby` ids, label id, invalid, status and required state and the form's size, variant and disabled, as signals; inside a field it registers the control, with or without `andesFormControl`, and only the field's first control takes its id. `andesIdRefs()` merges id lists, `provideAndesFieldBoundary()` hides the field from the controls a composite renders, and the field exposes `primaryControl()`.

Add AndesSegmented (single-choice segmented control with a sliding thumb, radiogroup keyboard navigation, options as data or projected `andes-segmented-option`, and forms support) plus AndesToggle and AndesToggleGroup (pressed toggle buttons, single or multiple selection, joined or spaced).

AndesSegmented gains `status` (`error`, which also sets `aria-invalid` and keeps the `aria-invalid` input working, or `warning`), `variant` (`default`, `outlined`, `filled`, `borderless`), `readOnly` (focusable and announced through `aria-readonly`, no hover feedback, the arrows move focus but no longer select), `motion` (`{ duration, easing }` for the sliding thumb, also available as the `--andes-segmented-duration` / `--andes-segmented-easing` properties), `scrollable` (the track scrolls horizontally, or vertically with `vertical`, keeps the selected and the keyboard-active option in view and fades each edge that has more behind it), `loop` (default true; Home/End jump to the first and last enabled option), `columns` (an equal-width wrapped grid, with `block`), `autoFocus`, `focus()` / `blur()` and `focused` / `blurred` outputs for the group as a whole. Options accept a `description`: a muted second line linked with `aria-describedby` that makes every segment grow to fit it.

AndesToggle gains `iconStart` / `iconEnd` templates (and projected `[slot=icon-start]` / `[slot=icon-end]`), `status` and `readOnly`; a toggle with no text, `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby` or `tooltip` logs a development warning once. AndesToggleGroup gains `loop` (default true, RTL-aware, with Home/End), `status`, `readOnly`, `required` (a single group cannot be emptied and Angular forms see `{ required }`), `name` (one hidden input per pressed value), `minCount` / `maxCount` for `type="multiple"` (unpressed toggles are disabled at the maximum, with a `limitReached` output carrying `{ limit, count, value }`), a `selectionChange` output carrying `{ value, added, removed }` for user changes (`valueChange` still belongs to the `value` model), `focus()` / `blur()` and `focused` / `blurred` outputs. `aria-required`, `aria-readonly` and `aria-invalid` are not allowed on the group and button roles, so they surface as `data-required`, `data-readonly` and `data-status`. The `outline` variant already existed; a card variant would duplicate it and is not added. The toggle markup gains `.andes-toggle__icon` and `.andes-toggle__label` wrappers (the label takes no box of its own) and the joined group now finds its end toggles by element type.

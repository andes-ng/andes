Added the popover and the popconfirm. `AndesPopover` is a non-modal panel anchored to a trigger: `click`, `hover`, `focus` and `contextMenu` triggers (alone or combined, and an empty list for a panel the page opens itself), twelve placements (or `side` and `align`) that flip to the opposite side when the panel does not fit, an arrow that can point at the centre of the trigger, `openDelay` and `closeDelay`, a two-way `open` model with `defaultOpen`, `openChange` and `openChangeDetail` (the new state and its cause: click, hover, focus, contextMenu, escape-key, outside-click, close-button, confirm, cancel or imperative), `show()`, `close()` and `toggle()`, `closeOnEscape`, `closeOnOutsideClick`, `autoFocus` and `restoreFocus`, `disabled`, `destroyOnHidden`, `zIndex`, `panelClass`, `width`, `maxWidth` and a scrolling `maxHeight`. On a trigger that wraps a control (an input, a select, a button) the panel is anchored to the whole outer box of the control, never over its padding or icons. With both `hover` and `click` triggers a hover preview turns into a pinned panel on a click: the same panel stays mounted, the content swaps (the templates and functions given to `title` and `content` receive `mode`, `'hover'` or `'click'`) with a fade and a smooth height change, and it then closes only on Close, a second click, an outside press or Escape. The panel is a labelled dialog that does not trap focus and returns it to the trigger on close, and popovers nest: Escape closes the inner panel first and a press inside it does not close the outer one.

The panel takes a `title` and a `content` as text, a template or a function (a template receives `let-close`), or is composed from `andesPopoverHeader`, `andesPopoverTitle`, `andesPopoverDescription` and `andesPopoverClose`. `color` takes a semantic preset or any CSS colour with an automatic text colour, `classNames` and `styles` (objects or functions of the popover props) reach the `root`, `arrow`, `title` and `body` parts, `container` draws the panel inside a given element (so a theme scope or `dir` on it applies), and the `--andes-popover-*` custom properties restyle one panel from CSS.

The surface follows the same slim scale as the alert and the toast: a 12px by 16px padding measured to the outer edge, an 8px radius, a soft layered shadow and a neutral border, a 14px/20px medium title over 13px/20px muted text, a soft 200ms entry (a fade, a 6px drift from the trigger side and a 0.97 scale growing from the arrow tip, on a long ease-out) and a quicker 140ms exit that keeps the panel mounted until it ends and reverses cleanly when the pointer comes back (`--andes-popover-duration`, `--andes-popover-exit-duration`, `--andes-popover-ease`, `--andes-popover-exit-ease`, `--andes-popover-distance` and `--andes-popover-scale` change it; reduced motion keeps only an 80ms fade), a 16px by 8px arrow with a rounded tip and curved shoulders whose outline continues the panel border, centred on the trigger on whole device pixels and kept clear of the rounded corners, and a width capped to the window minus 12px on each side so nothing overflows on a phone. Text, muted text and every colour preset keep WCAG AA contrast on the light and the dark theme.

`AndesPopconfirm` is a small confirmation bubble with the same placement, triggers, arrow, `open` model and focus options (placement `top` with an arrow by default), a `title` and a `description` as text, a template or a function, an `icon` that is a lucide icon name, a template or `null`, `okText`, `cancelText`, `okType`, `cancelType`, `okDisabled`, `okLoading`, `showCancel`, `okButtonProps` and `cancelButtonProps` (variant, size, colour, `ariaLabel`, class and more), `initialFocus` (Cancel by default, or OK), and an `onConfirm` and an `onCancel` that may return a promise: the button shows a loading state and ignores further presses, the bubble closes when the promise resolves and stays open, with focus back on the button, when it rejects. `actionsOn="click"` shows the buttons only once a click has pinned the panel, so a hover previews the question and a click asks it. It reports `confirm`, `cancelled` (also for Escape) and `popupClick`, and its parts take `classNames` and `styles` (`root`, `arrow`, `icon`, `title`, `body`, `footer`). The panel is a labelled and described alert dialog with a 14px/20px title, a 13px description and small (24px, 13px) buttons. The default icon and every icon in the stories are lucide, drawn through `ng-icon`.

Toasts now match Ant Design's sizes. A notification card is 384px wide with 20px/24px padding, an 8px radius, a 24px icon, a 16px title over a 14px description, and a 22px close button pinned to the top-end corner; a message has 9px/12px padding and a 16px icon. Cards move in 200ms on an ease-in-out curve (`--andes-toast-duration` overrides it), sliding 64px in from the side edge. Expanded toasts, messages included, are 16px apart, a collapsed deck scales each card down 6% and shows `stackPeek` px of it (default 8), and a collapsed message deck shows only the newest message.

AndesToastService and AndesMessageService gain `classNames` and `styles` per part (`root`, `wrapper`, `icon`, `content`, `title`, `description`, `actions`, `close`), `actionsTemplate` for your own buttons in the actions row and `headless` to replace the toast's whole content. Template contexts gain `close()`. AndesToastViewport gains `hotkey` (default `Alt+T`, moves focus to the newest toast; Escape dismisses it and returns focus) and `container` (moves the viewport into another element). A region holding toasts is now a labelled landmark, and every countdown holds while the tab is hidden.
